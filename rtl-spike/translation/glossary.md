## Turret and unit NAMES
Turrets and units are proper names: transliterate them into Arabic letters (e.g. Duo → دوو, Dagger → داغر, Flare → فلير, Mono → مونو, Poly → بولي, Mega → ميغا, Alpha → ألفا), do not translate their meaning.
